# Juan Pablo Gonzalez Camera

### Senior AI & Systems Engineer

I build reliable AI systems that connect models to real workflows through **explicit tool boundaries, structured validation, bounded retries, human approval, observability, security controls, and maintainable backend architecture**.

My engineering focus is **Engineering Certainty**: moving AI and automation systems beyond impressive demos toward workflows that can be validated, observed, operated, and safely recovered when failures occur.

> Public repositories are controlled engineering demonstrations or open-source contributions. They document architecture, trade-offs, tests, failure modes, and operational decisions without exposing client-confidential information.

## Engineering principles

```text
Validate inputs and outputs.
Make tool boundaries explicit.
Keep retries bounded and failures observable.
Require human approval for high-impact actions.
Use synthetic data and controlled adapters in public demonstrations.
Document trade-offs, constraints, and recovery paths.
```
